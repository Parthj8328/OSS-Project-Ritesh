# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone contributing to this project.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful and polite to everyone.
- Welcome different opinions and ideas.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Help beginners and new contributors.
- Accept feedback positively.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Insulting or offensive comments.
- Trolling or personal attacks.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.

## Reporting Guidelines

If you experience or notice unacceptable behaviour, report it to the repository maintainer through GitHub.

All reports will be reviewed fairly and handled respectfully.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove inappropriate content or take suitable action against contributors who violate this Code of Conduct.
